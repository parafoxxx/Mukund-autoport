How I built it, and the voice part

Built with Wispr Flow + Claude Code. As of 5 Nov 2026 my Flow dashboard shows 2,953 words dictated at 160 words a minute (Top 0.1%), 99% of it as AI prompts (85 dictated prompts). Inside the ride, voice is a control, not a gimmick: you can drive the auto by saying “move”, “left”, “faster” or “take me to NoBroker” (English or Hindi), the avatar speaks 20 recorded Hinglish lines, and a “Say it, see it” billboard types what you say and shows your speaking speed.

My first 90 days at Wispr Flow India

Press reports put India at #2 for Wispr Flow by users, but only a small slice of in-app revenue. The job is to grow those users and turn them into paying ones.

  1. “Bol ke likho”, a Hinglish creator programme: 50 micro-creators film type-vs-speak in Hinglish, paid per user who actually dictates in week one, not per view. Measure: cost per activated user, by creator.
  2. Campus ambassadors, timed to placement season: “dictate your SOP” sessions at IITs and NITs, plus a voice-only case-competition track. Measure: weekly active users per campus, referrals per ambassador.
  3. Price it for India, test on one campus first: a rupee student plan on UPI AutoPay plus a telecom or laptop bundle, tested for a month against a control campus. Measure: free-to-paid conversion.
